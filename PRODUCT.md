# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static site with no build step: HTML, CSS and ES modules in `tablero/`, libraries from a CDN at pinned versions (MapLibre GL, GSAP). Published to GitHub Pages from this repo through a GitHub Actions workflow that uploads only `tablero/` (decision 26). The data in `tablero/data/*.json` is generated by the Python pipeline (`scripts/09_semaforos.py`, `scripts/10_tablero.py`). The owner works in Python, so there is no Node toolchain to maintain.

## Users

- **Primary:** the head of the Secretaría de Tránsito y Transporte de Valledupar (STTV). He received the signal-timing plans of the SAST intersections as a table and said he did not understand the cycles. He will see the dashboard in three situations:
  - projected in a meeting, with Santiago driving;
  - alone on his computer;
  - on his phone, from a link sent by chat or email.
- **Secondary:** the STTV technical team, who need the exact timings and the methodology.
- The site is public, so anyone can open it. Texts are written for the boss and the technical team: clear technical Spanish (plan, ciclo, fase, todo rojo) with a glossary, not for the general public.

## Product Purpose

Make the traffic-signal programming at the intersections with SAST cameras understandable at a glance:
- when each plan runs;
- how long a cycle lasts;
- who gets green and for how long;
- why the night plans are shorter.

It also shows where the 15 authorized SAST cameras are and the official baseline indicators of the 9 operating ones.

**Success:** the boss watches an intersection and can explain its cycle in his own words. The presentation should impress enough to be shown in meetings.

## Positioning

Built on the real programming read from the SISTRA controller reports, not on assumed timings. Each signal group is tied to its approach through the SDM Bogotá trajectory codification (decision 30) and the direction each SAST camera monitors.

## Operating Context

- 5 signalized intersections: La Viña, Mercado Público, Los Manguitos, Colegio Loperena and Universidad Área Andina.
- 4 plans per controller, chosen by time of day and day type (weekday, Sunday, public holiday).
- Clock: America/Bogota time; Colombian public holidays under Ley 51/1983.
- The cycle phase relative to the real controller is unknown, so the live view is labeled as an illustrative phase.
- Vehicles and pedestrians in the simulation are illustrative, not traffic counts.

## Capabilities and Constraints

**Capabilities**
- Map of the signals and SAST cameras.
- Per-intersection cycle simulation.
- Plan in force by clock.
- Weekly schedule.
- KPIs and an auto-generated plain-language explanation.
- SAST record with codes and baseline.
- Presentation mode.

**Constraints**
- Signal timings come from the controllers, not from the email (decision 27).
- The group→approach mapping is animated only once Santiago has validated it (decision 28).
- How signals look on the street (decision 29):
  - preparation (TIRA–TIV) is shown as red;
  - the pedestrian clearance second is flashing red;
  - every intersection has a countdown timer.
- No personal data is published.

## Brand Commitments

- No logos or official imagery. The institution's name appears in text only: «Secretaría de Tránsito y Transporte de Valledupar».
- Voice: technical, clear, institutional Spanish.

## Evidence on Hand

- `data/processed/semaforos.json`: plans, schedules, compatibility matrices and OSM geometry.
- `outputs/capas/equipos_sast.geojson`: 15 SAST cameras.
- The «Resumen» sheet of `outputs/2026-10-04_sttv_linea-base-sast_plataforma-ansv.xlsx`: the official baseline (+15 m).
- Santiago's signage inventory maps, for crosswalks and the yellow box.

There are no traffic counts, real-time feeds or controller offsets. Do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Explain before impressing: every animation must make the cycle easier to understand.
2. Field truth: what is drawn matches the controller and the street; anything illustrative says so.
3. Traceability: every figure can be traced to its source (PDF, Excel, OSM) and decision.
4. Works projected, on a desk and on a phone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Signal states are never encoded by color alone: shape, label and position carry them too.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Text must stay legible on a projector.
